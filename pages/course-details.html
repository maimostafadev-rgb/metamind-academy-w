Cybersecurity Professional Program

Course Overview

A comprehensive, hands-on cybersecurity program designed to take students from the fundamentals of cybersecurity to a job-ready professional level. Students learn how to secure systems and networks, identify vulnerabilities, analyze threats, monitor security events, respond to incidents, and perform ethical security testing in controlled environments.

Course Requirements

  • Basic computer skills
  • Basic knowledge of operating systems
  • Basic understanding of computer networks
  • Basic English language skills
  • A laptop or desktop computer
  • Stable internet connection
  • Willingness to learn and practice
  • No previous cybersecurity experience is required
